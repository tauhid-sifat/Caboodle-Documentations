@Md Mashiul Azam Chowdhury EftiI have created an endpoint to get the Products for Banner SKU creation process. Here’s the url: `api/v1/productspecs/for-bannersku/{bannerID}` for example: `api/v1/productspecs/for-bannersku/055e32d2-008c-47f4-985c-556aec063ead`.



In the response of the endpoint you will get list of Products, each will have 3 properties (ID, Name, ShouldBeDisabled). You can show or disable the specific product using the ShouldBeDisabled bool property.
