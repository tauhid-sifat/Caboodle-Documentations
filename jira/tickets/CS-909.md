## Description

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Go to the distributor details page.
2. Open the “Add Product Price” modal.
3. Try to add pricing for a SKU with status inactive and others (not active or continue).
4. Click the date field.
**Expected Behavior:**
Date field should be disabled for inactive SKUs.
**Actual Behavior:**
Date field is clickable even for inactive SKUs.
