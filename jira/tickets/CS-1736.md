## Description

**Description:**
When a promo event is deleted from a promotion in the Trade Spend Sandbox, the corresponding promo case information still continues to appear in the Monthly Report. The deletion is not being reflected/propagated to the Monthly Report data.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Navigate to Promotional Planning → Trade Spend Sandbox.
2. Open a promo that has an associated Monthly Report entry.
3. Delete a promo event from the Sandbox.
4. Navigate to the Monthly Report for the same period/promo.
5. Check whether the deleted event's case information still appears.

**Expected Result:**
Once a promo event is deleted from the Sandbox, its corresponding case information should also be removed from (or excluded from) the Monthly Report.

**Actual Result:**
The deleted promo event's case information still appears in the Monthly Report, even though it no longer exists in the Sandbox.
