## Description

When an Admin deactivates a Sub-User from the Admin panel, that user's email should no longer appear in the recipient-related lists — specifically the **Notify Mail** list and the **Sent Mail** list — within both **Category Review** and **Brand Report** sections. Currently, inactive sub-users' emails continue to show up in these lists.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Log in as Admin.
2. Deactivate a Sub-User from the Admin panel.
3. Go to Category Review section > check Notify Mail list and Sent Mail list.
4. Go to Brand Report section > check Notify Mail list and Sent Mail list.
5. Observe that the deactivated sub-user's email still appears in these lists.

**Expected Behavior:**
Once a sub-user is marked inactive by Admin, their email should be excluded from:

* Notify Mail list (Category Review)
* Sent Mail list (Brand Report)

**Actual Behavior:**
Inactive sub-user's email continues to appear in both Notify Mail and Sent Mail lists across both sections.
