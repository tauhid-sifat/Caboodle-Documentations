## Description

Multiple changes required to KPI cards in the Brand Environment:

1. Move the KPI cards from the **Category Review Report** to the **Category Calendar**.
2. Remove 3 specific KPI cards after the move.
3. Fix KPI card filtering, which is currently non-functional (clicking a status doesn't filter the Calendar).

1. **Move KPI Cards**

* Relocate all existing KPI cards from **Brand Environment > Category Review Report** to **Brand Environment > Category Calendar**.

2. **Remove KPI Cards (after move, in Category Calendar)**


Remove the following 3 cards entirely:

* Submission Due Dates
* Upcoming Brand Reviews
* Next Action

**Keep the following 4 cards:**

* Submission Status
* Brand Response
* Waiting On
* Review Result

3. **Fix KPI Card Filtering (currently broken)**

* Currently, clicking a status option within a KPI card does **not** filter anything (e.g., Submission Status card shows "5 In Progress," but clicking "In Progress" does nothing).
* **Expected:** Clicking a status icon/option (e.g., "In Progress" under Submission Status) should filter the Category Calendar to show only Reviews matching that status.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* KPI cards no longer appear on Category Review Report; they now appear on Category Calendar.
* Only 4 cards remain: Submission Status, Brand Response, Waiting On, Review Result.
* Submission Due Dates, Upcoming Brand Reviews, and Next Action cards are fully removed.
* Clicking any status option within a KPI card filters the Category Calendar to matching Reviews.
* Filtered results accurately match the count shown on the KPI card at time of click.
