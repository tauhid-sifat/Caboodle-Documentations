## Comments

### Minhaz (2023-11-12T19:36:36.989+0600)

@Constance Wolfe , We looked into what was causing this problem. The admin submitted a price list for Brand "Cocojune" on November 2nd, 2023. At the time, our system did not verify whether the brand had a distributor connected or not. When you uploaded a price list at the time, it was just uploaded into the system without any additional checks. 

However, after November 5th, we changed the pricing list import. We implemented a duplicate check as well as a distributor check. Currently, if you enter a distributor ID while uploading a price list, the system checks to see if that distributor has a connection with the specified brand. If it is linked, we permit uploading; otherwise, we do not. 

You are seeing the distributor "KEHE FOB" under the category "Kids Pouches" in the "Cocojune" brand pricing list since the data was uploaded before November 6th. And this verification was not included at the time. 

Now, when uploading a price list from the admin side, do not include the distributor ID if the distributor is not associated with that brand. You are free to leave it blank.  Later, In the brand environment you can add distributors with the price list If you want  by editing price list.

Finally, I have a question: What should we do with the pricing list that includes a distributor that has no connection with the respective brand? Should we connect the distributor to the brand through the brand environment, or should these pricing lists be removed?
