**Title:** Allow notifying Brand Contacts + skip deactivated users on resend

**Type:** Feature Enhancement
**Component:** Category Review — Notify/Re-notify (Recipient Selection)

**Description:**

**Background:**
Today, when a broker notifies a brand about a Category Review, they can only pick recipients from the brand's Owner and Sub-users. Brand Contacts — people manually added to a brand's contact list who often don't have a login to the system — can't be notified at all. Separately, when a broker resends a past notification, it goes out exactly as before, even if one of the original recipients has since had their account deactivated.

**What this change does:**

1. The recipient list for notifying a brand now also includes that brand's Contacts, shown alongside its Owner and Sub-users — so brokers can choose to include them.
2. Recipients are now picked by name/email rather than an internal system ID, which is what makes it possible to include Contacts (they have no login account to reference).
3. When resending a past notification, the system now automatically leaves out anyone whose account has since been deactivated. Addresses that were never tied to a system account (like a Contact) are unaffected and still receive the resend.

**What did not change:**

* No new database tables or storage — this only changes how recipients are selected and checked.
* Leaving the recipient selection blank still defaults to Owner + Sub-users only; Contacts are never included automatically, only when specifically chosen.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* Brand Contacts show up in the recipient picker, alongside Owner and Sub-users.
* Selecting a Contact and sending a notification successfully emails them.
* Leaving recipients blank still only notifies Owner + Sub-users (Contacts stay opt-in).
* Resending a past notification skips any recipient whose account is now deactivated.
* Existing notify/resend behavior for Owners and Sub-users is unaffected.

**Frontend Impact:**
The Notify/Re-notify screens need to send the new recipient format, and the recipient picker needs to display and allow selecting from the new "Contacts" group.
