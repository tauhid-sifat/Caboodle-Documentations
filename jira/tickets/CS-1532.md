## Description

After successfully uploading product prices via **Price Upload**, the newly uploaded prices do not automatically appear in the price list. Users have to manually **refresh the page** or **switch tabs and come back** for the updated values to show up.

**Impact**

* Users may think the upload failed or didn't take effect, since the list appears unchanged immediately after upload — even though the success modal (see related tickets) indicates completion.
* Creates unnecessary confusion and extra manual steps (refresh/tab-switch) for users to confirm their upload actually worked.
* Poor perceived reliability of the price upload feature, even though the underlying data is correctly saved.

**Steps to Reproduce**

1. Navigate to the **Pricing** section.
2. Note the current price list/values for a set of products.
3. Perform a **Price Upload** with valid data for those products.
4. Wait for the success confirmation (modal).
5. Check the price list without refreshing — observe the newly uploaded prices are **not reflected**.
6. Refresh the page or switch to another tab and back — observe the prices now appear correctly.

**Expected Behavior**

* After a successful price upload, the price list should automatically update/re-fetch to reflect the newly uploaded values, without requiring a manual refresh or tab switch.

**Actual Behavior**

* The price list remains stale/unchanged after upload until the user manually refreshes the page or switches tabs.
