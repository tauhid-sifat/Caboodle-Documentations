## Description

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Go to the **Contact** page
2. Click **Add Contact**
3. Fill in the form and click **Create**
4. Reopen the **Add Contact** modal

**Actual Result:**
The form retains the previous data instead of resetting to empty fields.

**Expected Result:**
After creating a contact, the modal form should reset all fields to empty when reopened.
