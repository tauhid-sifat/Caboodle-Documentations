## Description

* When a user selects a due-date range such as **Urgent — 7 Days** or **Soon — 14 Days**, automatically filter the Brand Report list based on the selected range.
* Ensure only brands/reports whose due dates fall within the selected range are displayed.
* Update the report list immediately when the due-date range changes.
* Verify the filtering works correctly with other Brand Report filters.
