## Description

* **steps to reproduce**:1. go to a distributor's details page
  2. click on any available banner on the left side (e.g., "abby’s")
  3. observe that the right section loads correct banner details (products, activities, rules)
  4. now search for a banner that does not exist for this distributor
  5. left panel shows “no banner found for this distributor”
* **expected result**:
  the right-side details panel should clear and show a blank/default state
* **actual result**:
  the previous banner's details (products, activities, rules) remain visible on the right side, which causes confusion
