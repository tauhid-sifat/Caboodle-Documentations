## Description

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Go to the Product module.
2. Edit a product and change its category.
3. Navigate to the Pricing page for that product.
**Expected Result:**
Pricing data should still display correctly for the product after category change.
**Actual Result:**
Pricing shows **"pricing not set"** after changing the product category.
