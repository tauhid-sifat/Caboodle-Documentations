## Description

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Open Distributor module.
2. Navigate to a distributor with no banners.
3. Check the banner list modal.
**Expected Behavior:**
"Select All" should be inactive or hidden when no banners are available.
**Actual Behavior:**
"Select All" appears active and shows as selected even though no items exist.
