## Description

In both Email Activity and Email History sections, all email records are currently being categorized under **"Notify Brand"** for the "Method of Entry" field/filter, regardless of the actual method through which the entry/email was triggered. This results in inaccurate categorization and makes the "Method of Entry" filter effectively non-functional, since every record falls under the same value.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Go to Email Activity section.
2. Filter or review records by "Method of Entry."
3. Observe that all email records show as "Notify Brand," regardless of how they were actually triggered/sent.
4. Repeat the same check in Email History section — same issue observed.

**Expected Behavior:**
Each email record's "Method of Entry" should accurately reflect the actual method/source that triggered it (e.g., Notify Brand, Notify, Re-Notify, or other applicable methods — please confirm the full list of valid methods, excluding "Manual" per the earlier ticket). Filtering by "Method of Entry" should correctly return only records matching that specific method.

**Actual Behavior:**
All records are incorrectly tagged/displayed as "Notify Brand," regardless of true origin.
