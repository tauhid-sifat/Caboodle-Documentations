## Description

When a brand sub-user is invited and accepts the invitation, they are expected to appear in the Notify Mail section of the Broker Onboarding page as an available/notifiable recipient. However, if a broker already has that page open when a new sub-user is assigned and accepts, the new sub-user does not appear in the list. The broker must manually refresh the page to see them.
