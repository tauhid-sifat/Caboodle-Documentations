## Description

In the Distributor Sales Report section, within the **Sales by Chain** table, two summary totals are inaccurate:

* **Total Sales Dollars** does not match the sum of individual row-level Sales Dollars values.
* **Total Points Of Distribution Count** does not match the sum of individual row-level Points Of Distribution Count values.

**Impact**

* Users cannot trust either total for business reporting, forecasting, or performance tracking.
* Multiple incorrect metrics in the same table compounds risk if this data is exported, shared, or feeds into other dashboards/decisions.
* Reinforces a pattern of totals-mismatch issues across report sections, raising broader concern about the reliability of aggregation logic platform-wide.

**Steps to Reproduce**

1. Navigate to Distributor Sales Report section.
2. Open the **Sales by Chain** table.
3. Manually sum the **Sales Dollars** column across all visible rows; compare against **Total Sales Dollars**.
4. Manually sum the **Points Of Distribution Count** column across all visible rows; compare against **Total Points Of Distribution Count**.
5. Observe mismatches in both totals.

**Expected Behavior**

* Total Sales Dollars should equal the exact sum of the Sales Dollars values across all rows.
* Total Points Of Distribution Count should equal the exact sum of the Points Of Distribution Count values across all rows.

**Actual Behavior**

* Both totals differ from the sum of their respective row-level values.
