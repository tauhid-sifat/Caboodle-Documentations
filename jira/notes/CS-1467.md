## Description

**Depends on:** [CS-1462](https://caboodleservices.atlassian.net/browse/CS-1462)

Move the existing team-listing and member-management screens onto Organization membership. Ticket 1 introduced replacements for several of these, so part of this ticket is retiring screens rather than converting them — check with front-end first, as anything already unused can simply be removed.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* An Admin cannot view, deactivate, or remove a member of a different Organization.
* The pending-invitations list is correct per Organization.
* Deactivating a member works and is reflected in the listings.
* Removing a member behaves as defined in Ticket 1 — access revoked and sign-in disabled, in a single step that either fully succeeds or fully fails.
* Any screen removed rather than converted is confirmed unused by the front end first.
