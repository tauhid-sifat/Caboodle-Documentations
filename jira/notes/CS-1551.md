## Description

When adding a new Promo event in TSS, on Step 4 of Promo Creation, the "Comments" field/section does not fit properly within the visible window. This may cause content to be cut off, require unwanted scrolling, or break the layout, affecting usability during promo creation.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Go to TSS section.
2. Start creating a new Promo event.
3. Proceed through the steps to Step 4.
4. Observe the Comments field/section — it does not fit within the window (overflow, cut-off, or layout break).

**Expected Behavior:**
The Comments field should fit properly within the modal/window at Step 4, with appropriate scrolling (if needed) or responsive sizing so all content and the field itself are fully visible and usable.

**Actual Behavior:**
The Comments field/section overflows or doesn't fit within the window, causing a display issue.
