## Description

### Steps to Reproduce:

1. Navigate to TSS Stepper
2. Open a dropdown field (e.g., add sku)
3. Scroll the page or modal
4. Observe the dropdown position

---

### Actual Result:

* Dropdown box moves from its original aligned position
* Dropdown appears floating/misaligned during scroll

---

### Expected Result:

* Dropdown should remain fixed/aligned with its input field
* Dropdown position should not move unexpectedly while scrolling
