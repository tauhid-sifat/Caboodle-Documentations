## Description

Today, notifying a brand about a Category Review can result in duplicate emails being sent if the same action is triggered more than once for the same brand and review. There's also no easy way to see who has already been notified for a report, or to review a history of every notification email that's gone out. Resending an email currently allows the content to be edited, which creates risk of sending an email that doesn't match what was originally recorded.
