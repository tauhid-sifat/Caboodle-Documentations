## Description

When a user removes a product from a banner, that product should immediately become available to add again. Instead, if the user opens the Add Product modal right after removal, the product still appears as already bound/added to the banner. The user has to manually refresh the page for the product to show as free/available.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Open a banner that has an associated product.
2. Remove the product from the banner.
3. Without refreshing, open the Add Product modal in the Product List.
4. Search for or locate the just-removed product.

**Expected Behavior:**
Immediately after removal, the product should appear as available (unbound) in the Add Product modal, with no page refresh needed.

**Actual Behavior:**
The product still shows as already added/bound in the Add Product modal until the user manually refreshes the page.
