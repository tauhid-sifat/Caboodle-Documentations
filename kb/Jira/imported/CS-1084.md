## Description

Products added from the “Product Specification > Products” page are supposed to be deletable. But, when attempting to delete them, a pop-up error message is shown stating that the product is linked to item(s), even though the product was just created.

**Steps to reproduce:**

1. Log in to the Caboodle service website
2. Select a brand
3. Navigate to **Management>Product Specification>Products** page
4. Click **Add Product** and create a new product.
5. Search for the newly created product.
6. Attempt to delete the product.

**Expected Result:** The product should be successfully deleted, and a success pop-up should appear with the message: **“Product deleted successfully“.**
**Actual Result:** The product is not deleted, and an error pop-up appears with the message: **“Selected Products can not be deleted. They are linked to item(s)“.**
