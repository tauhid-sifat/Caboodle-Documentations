## Description

When adding a new Promo event in TSS, the Product List dropdown does not remain anchored/stuck to its parent modal. As the modal scrolls or the dropdown opens, the Product List moves off-screen, making it difficult or impossible to view and select items from the list.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Go to TSS section.
2. Start creating a new Promo event ("Add a Promo Event").
3. Open the Product List dropdown field.
4. Scroll within the modal or interact with the page.
5. Observe that the Product List dropdown detaches from its field/trigger and moves off-screen instead of staying anchored to the modal.

**Expected Behavior:**
The Product List dropdown should remain anchored ("stick") to its input field within the modal at all times, regardless of scrolling, and stay fully visible within the viewport/modal bounds.

**Actual Behavior:**
The dropdown moves off-screen and loses its position relative to the modal, breaking the selection experience.
