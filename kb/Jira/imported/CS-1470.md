## Description

**Depends on:** [CS-1462](https://caboodleservices.atlassian.net/browse/CS-1462)

Switch over the screens people use to move around the product — the brand switcher and module list — plus the screens for assigning brands to people.

This ticket also **removes automatic brand sharing**. Today, giving a brand to a Broker silently gives it to everyone beneath them. Under Organizations everyone is a peer, so access goes only to the people actually chosen. Admins can still select several people at once, so it's no more effort day to day, but becomes explicit and visible.

The brand switcher should also get noticeably faster.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* Every role sees the correct brands in their brand switcher: Admin, Broker, Broker Sub User, and both brand-side roles.
* Each role sees the modules appropriate to them.
* Giving someone a brand grants it to exactly the people chosen and nobody else.
* An Admin can give a brand to several members in a single action.
* Brand-side users are entirely unaffected.
* The brand switcher is measurably no slower, and is expected to be faster.
