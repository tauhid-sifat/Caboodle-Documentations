## Description

In the "Add New Promo" flow within Trade Spend Sandbox (Select Retailer & Banner → Select Product step), a product cannot be selected if it has no pricing set ("No Pricing Found"). The user is able to add pricing for the product directly from this screen via the "Add Product Pricing" modal (Distributors, Products, Per Unit Price, Valid From → Create).

However, after successfully adding the price, the product list/selection screen does not update to reflect the new pricing — the product still appears as "No Pricing Found" and remains unselectable. A manual page refresh is required for the updated pricing to reflect and for the product to become selectable.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Navigate to Promotional Planning → Trade Spend Sandbox → Add New Promo.
2. Select a Retailer and Banner.
3. On the Products step, locate a product showing "No Pricing Found."
4. Attempt to select it — note that selection is blocked.
5. Use the "Add Product Pricing" option to add a Distributor, Per Unit Price, and Valid From date for that product, then click "Create."
6. Without refreshing, check the product's pricing status and try to select it again.

**Expected Result:**
After successfully adding pricing for a product, the product list should update immediately (without requiring a manual refresh) to reflect the new price, and the product should become selectable right away.

**Actual Result:**
After adding pricing, the product still shows "No Pricing Found" and remains unselectable until the user manually refreshes the page.
