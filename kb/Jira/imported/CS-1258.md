**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Go to TSS.
2. Open any existing promo in View & Edit mode.
3. Click on the date edit field repeatedly or quickly multiple times.

**Actual Result:**

**Expected Result:**

* Date edit should remain stable regardless of multiple clicks and should not trigger any application error.
