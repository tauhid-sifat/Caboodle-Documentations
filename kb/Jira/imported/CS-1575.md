## Description

In CRM – Distributors, there is a "Distributor Name" field. However, in CRM – Distributor Delivery Methods, the "Distributor Name" label is actually showing the **Parent Distributor** name, while the **Delivery Method Name** is also being labeled/displayed as "Distributor Name." This creates confusion and makes it difficult to differentiate between the two distinct fields.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Navigate to CRM – Distributors; note "Distributor Name" field.
2. Navigate to CRM – Distributor Delivery Methods.
3. Observe that "Distributor Name" here actually represents the Parent Distributor.
4. Observe that Delivery Method Name is also displayed/labeled as "Distributor Name."

**Expected Behavior:**
Each field should be clearly and distinctly labeled:

* Parent distributor reference → labeled as **"Distributor Name"**
* Delivery method's own name → labeled as **"Delivery Method Name"**, not "Distributor Name."
