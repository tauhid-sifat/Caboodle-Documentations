## Description

When a Broker sends a "Notify Brand" email from Category Review, the "From" address should always be fixed as **[info@caboodleservices.com](mailto:info@caboodleservices.com)**, regardless of who the logged-in Broker/user is.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Log in as a Broker.
2. Navigate to Category Review.
3. Trigger "Notify Brand" for any entry/brand.
4. Check the "From" address on the email received by the brand.

**Expected Result:**
The "From" address on all Notify Brand emails should always show as [info@caboodleservices.com](mailto:info@caboodleservices.com).
