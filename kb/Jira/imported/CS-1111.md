## Description

When creating a promo in TSS, if the process is paused and any assigned category/product is updated or deleted before resuming, Step 2 of the promo creation wizard displays a blank state. Even after a hard refresh, the data does not appear.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Start creating a new promo in TSS.
2. Assign a category/product in Step 2.
3. Pause the promo creation (save as draft).
4. Update or delete the assigned category/product from the system.
5. Resume the promo creation from the draft.

**Expected Result:**
Step 2 should display the updated category/product list or indicate that the assigned items have been removed, allowing the user to reassign. Deleted categories should also be excluded from the category list in step 2.

**Actual Result:**
Step 2 shows a blank state, and the data does not load even after a hard refresh.
