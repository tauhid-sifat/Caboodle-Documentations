## Description

In **Reports > Retail Report > Products**, the **Retailer Name** is correctly displayed in ascending alphabetical order. However, the **Banner List** is not following any consistent sorting order.

**Expected Behavior:**

* Both **Retailer Name** and **Banner List** should be displayed in **ascending alphabetical order** for consistency and better usability.

**Actual Behavior:**

* Retail List → Sorted in ascending order 
* Banner List → Not sorted / inconsistent 

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Navigate to **Reports > Retail Report > Products**
2. Observe the **Retail List** and **Banner List**
3. Compare the ordering of both lists
