## Description

Implement *read-only tables* to support the Market Overview functionality, providing users with a consolidated view of market data without allowing direct modifications. This feature will ensure that market insights are displayed efficiently while maintaining data integrity.

h3. Scope of Work:

* Design and implement tables optimized for read-only access to Market Overview data.
* Include relevant fields such as product, category, sales metrics, and other market indicators.
* Ensure efficient querying and pagination for large datasets.
* Integrate tables with the Market Overview UI for display purposes.
* Ensure proper access control so that only authorized users can view the data.
* Implement any necessary indexing or caching to improve performance.

h3. Acceptance Criteria:

* Read-only tables are implemented and populated with relevant market data.
* Users can view Market Overview data without modifying it.
* Queries are optimized for performance with large datasets.
* Access control is enforced for authorized users only.
