## Description

*Depends on:* [+CS-1462+|https://caboodleservices.atlassian.net/browse/CS-1462]+-+[+CS-1471+|https://caboodleservices.atlassian.net/browse/CS-1471]

The final switch-over. One central check decides whether a user may open a given brand, and this ticket moves it onto Organization-based access.

It is last because it applies to everything at once, including areas — reporting, dashboards, data uploads, product specs, stores, the trade spend sandbox and others — that rely entirely on this check and have no protection of their own.

*Acceptance Criteria:*

* Every role can open the brands they've been given, and only those.
* Attempting to open a brand the user hasn't been given is refused with a clear message.
* Every remaining area is tested with a Broker account and a Broker Sub User account — reporting, dashboards, data uploads, data hub, product specs, stores, intelligence and the trade spend sandbox.
* Brand-side users behave exactly as before.
* Someone whose access was removed is stopped at this check, not merely shown an empty screen.
* No noticeable slowdown — this check runs on nearly every request.
* The change can be reversed on its own if a problem is found after release.
