## Description

## Summary

A Simple Promotion cannot be deleted from the Approved Promotions section. The Simple Promotion details screen shows only **Export** and **Edit**. Standard approved promotions have a **Remove** button on their details screen (next to "Select Promo Options"), but Simple ones do not.

## Environment

Staging, brand **Apple Jacks**, Promotional Planning → Approved Promotions → any Simple Promotion (e.g. "EDLP = Y two months")

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Approved Promotions.
2. Open a Simple Promotion.
3. Look for a Remove or Delete option.

## Actual result

* There is no Remove button. Only Export and Edit are shown.
* The user has no way to delete a Simple Promotion.

## Expected result

* A **Remove** button appears on the Simple Promotion details screen, with the same position, style and confirmation dialog as the Standard approved promotion's Remove.
* On confirm:* The whole promotion is deleted and no longer appears in **Approved Promotions**.
  * All of its events, including EDLP, are removed from the **Brand promotional calendar** and the **Broker promotional calendar**, and from their exports.
* Reports are unaffected, since Simple Promotions are already excluded from them.
* Permissions match those for deleting a Standard promotion.

## Notes for dev

* The BE already supports this. Per the QA guide §10.7, `DELETE api/v1/tradespends` deletes a Simple Promotion normally. This should be FE-only: add the button and wire it to the existing delete call.
* After deleting, redirect the user to the Approved Promotions list and show a success message.

## QA retest points

* Open the deleted promotion's URL directly. It should show not found.
* Check that the promotion is gone from both calendars and their exports.
* Check the DB: the promotion and its events are soft-deleted.
* Duplicate a Simple Promotion, delete the copy, and check that the original is unaffected.
