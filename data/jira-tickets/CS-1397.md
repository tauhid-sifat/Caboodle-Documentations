## Description

In the brand assignment modal, users must first remove the existing assigned brand before assigning a new one. However, the current UI/UX does not guide users clearly through this flow.

Currently, the assigned brand section appears first and the delete/remove option is placed below it, which may confuse users about the required steps.

**Current Flow:**

* Assigned brand is displayed first
* Delete/remove option appears below
* User may not immediately understand that removing the existing brand is mandatory before assigning a new one

**Expected Improvement:**

* Show the delete/remove action more prominently at the beginning
* Disable the new brand assignment option while a brand is already assigned
* Once the existing brand is removed, enable the assign brand option automatically

**Expected Result:**
The modal should clearly guide users through the required sequence:

1. Remove existing brand
2. Assign new brand

**Impact:**
The current flow may cause confusion and reduce usability during brand reassignment operations.
