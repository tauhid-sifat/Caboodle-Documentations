## Description

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Open a distributor with no associated banners.
2. Check the product spec section.
**Expected Behavior:**
A clear message or empty state should be shown if no banners are linked.
**Actual Behavior:**
Product spec appears with unclear or confusing data, implying linkage where there is none.
