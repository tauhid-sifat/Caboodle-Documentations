## Description

"Valid From" date in pricing form appears one day earlier than the selected date.

* *Steps to Reproduce:*
*# Go to "Pricing" > "Add New Pricing".
*# Select a “Valid From” date (e.g., 05/05/2025).
*# Save the entry.
* *Actual Result:*
** The saved entry shows “Valid From” date as 04/05/2025 (one day earlier).
** When editing, the date field does not correctly display the previously saved date — it may show a blank.
* *Expected Result:*
** The date saved and shown should match the one selected by the user.
** The date should persist correctly and appear pre-filled when editing the pricing record.
