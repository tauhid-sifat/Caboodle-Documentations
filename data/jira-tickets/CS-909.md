## Description

*Steps to Reproduce:*

# Go to the distributor details page.
# Open the “Add Product Price” modal.
# Try to add pricing for a SKU with status inactive and others (not active or continue).
# Click the date field.
*Expected Behavior:*
Date field should be disabled for inactive SKUs.
*Actual Behavior:*
Date field is clickable even for inactive SKUs.
