## Description

The "Avg Weekly Sales Velocity" field in TSS is expected to round to a maximum of 2 decimal places. This works correctly for existing brands, but for newly added brands, the value displays with more than 2 decimals — indicating the rounding logic isn't being applied consistently at the point of calculation/entry for new records.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

- Go to TSS section.

- Check "Avg Weekly Sales Velocity" for an existing/older brand — value is correctly rounded to 2 decimals.

- Add a new brand to TSS.

- Check "Avg Weekly Sales Velocity" for the newly added brand.

- Observe the value displays with more than 2 decimal places.


**Expected Behavior:**
"Avg Weekly Sales Velocity" should always round to exactly 2 decimal places, regardless of when the brand was added.

**Actual Behavior:**
Newly added brands show more than 2 decimal places for this field, inconsistent with existing brand records.
