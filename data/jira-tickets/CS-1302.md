## Description

WFM PM Fee

Needs a little discussion to determine if this should be added in the **Promo Results Page** in the View & Edit Promo Event Details or the **Step 4 Metrics in the stepper**. I’m leaning towards the Promo Results Page and adding it to the Add Button.

The Goal is to allow the user to enter the fees that are associated with Whole Foods Amazon Prime which are applied to every Promotion. They are unique in how they are calculated. Whole Foods is the largest Natural Food Store in the US so while it’s unique to this Retail, 90% of brands sell to Whole Foods.

The Name of this option should be labeled **WFM Amazon Prime Fees**

I believe we need a pop-up box where the **User enters 3 inputs for calculations.**

1. **Prime Member Allowance (PMA) ** (calculation = Per unit allowance * redemption rate % * calculated units sold)
2. **Redemption Rate %** (No calculation for this, it’s used to calculate the PM Allowance)

3. **Per Unit Scan Fee** (Calculation = Unit scan fee (currently .02) * total calculated units sold on promotion. Redemption rate does not apply)

 

Notes:

·       All PMA fees apply only to Promoted Units

·       PMA fee should be matched to specific promotions so user would select the promotion to associate the PMA Fees to.  

·       The Fees should be included in the Trade Spend Calculations as part of the Store Ads/TPR Fees and listed separately in the AD Fee Filter

·       See sample WFM Planner Attached. In column AC (Total PRIME Redemption (Dollars)) you can see the calculation.

 List in Ad Fee in S**elect Promo Option Filter **as **WFM PMA** IF these items are added to the promotion.



There should be a new column called **WFM PMA Fees** in the events details next to Ad & TPR Fees (Red Box in screen shot)
