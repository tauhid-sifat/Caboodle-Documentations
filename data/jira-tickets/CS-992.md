## Description

* **title**: search keyword “ice” returns product with unrelated category “grocery”
* **steps to reproduce**:1. go to **retailer > banner details** page
  2. in the **products** tab, use the search bar to type **“ice”**
  3. observe the search results
* **expected result**:
  only products with “ice” in the product name, category, or relevant fields should be returned
* **actual result**:
  product with category **“grocery”** (e.g., rice 11g) is shown in the result, even though it does not match the keyword “ice”
