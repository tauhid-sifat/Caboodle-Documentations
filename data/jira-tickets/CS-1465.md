## Description

**Depends on:** [CS-1462](https://caboodleservices.atlassian.net/browse/CS-1462)

Switch Market Overview and Promotional Management over to Organization-based access. Fixes a real bug in both: removing someone's brand access doesn't currently take effect there — they keep seeing the brand's data. There's also an inconsistency where the Market Overview list and its own export disagree about what the same user should see.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

* Removing a member's brand access removes that brand's data from these screens immediately.
* Two Organizations working on the same brand cannot see each other's records.
* An unaffected user sees identical rows and counts before and after, in both areas.
