## Description

Today, notifying a brand about a Category Review can result in duplicate emails being sent if the same action is triggered more than once for the same brand and review. There's also no easy way to see who has already been notified for a report, or to review a history of every notification email that's gone out. Resending an email currently allows the content to be edited, which creates risk of sending an email that doesn't match what was originally recorded.

## What's Changing

1. **No more duplicate notifications** Notifying a brand about a Category Review it has already been notified about will no longer send another email or create a duplicate record. It will simply confirm the brand is already notified.
2. **New "Re-notify" action** When a broker genuinely wants to send a fresh reminder to a brand that's already been notified, they can do this explicitly through a dedicated re-notify action, sent to that brand's current owner and users.
3. **New "Recipients" view** Brokers can see, for any Brand Report, who the brand's owner and users are, and how many unique people have been notified about it so far.
4. **Simplified, safer resend** Resending a previously sent notification email is now a single, consistent action. The resent email will always be an exact copy of what was originally sent — content can no longer be changed before resending.
5. **New Email History view** Brokers can browse and filter a full history of every notification and resend email sent for their brands, including who it went to and when.

## Acceptance Criteria

* Notifying an already-notified brand does not send a new email or create a duplicate entry.
* A broker can re-notify an already-notified brand on demand, and the correct people receive it.
* A broker can view a brand's owner/users and how many people have been notified for a report.
* Resending an email always sends the exact original content, with no way to edit it beforehand.
* A broker can view and filter the full history of notification/resend emails for their brands only — never another broker's.
* Access to all of the above respects existing brand/broker permissions.
