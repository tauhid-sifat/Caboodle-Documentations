## Description

* *Steps to Reproduce:*
*# Go to the Contact page.
*# Click Add Contact.
*# Enter a contact with an email and phone number that already exist in the system.
*# Click Create.
* *Expected Result:*
** The system should prevent creation and display an error for duplicate email/phone.
* *Actual Result:*
** Duplicate contacts are allowed without any validation or warning.
