## Description

* **Steps to Reproduce:**1. Go to the Contact page.
  2. Click Add Contact.
  3. Enter a contact with an email and phone number that already exist in the system.
  4. Click Create.
* **Expected Result:*** The system should prevent creation and display an error for duplicate email/phone.
* **Actual Result:*** Duplicate contacts are allowed without any validation or warning.
