## Description

Retailer: product delete – second item triggers error after deleting first

* *steps to reproduce*:
*# go to a retailer’s product list
*# delete one product from the list
*# try deleting another product immediately afterward
* *expected result*:
each selected product should be deleted independently without issue
* *actual result*:
the second delete action mistakenly references the previously deleted sku, resulting in an error: “sku not found”
