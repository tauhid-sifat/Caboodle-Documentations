## Description

EDLP is showing up in the Promotional Calendar only in the month of January even though the dates of the EDLP are Jan 1-Dec. 31, 2026. The total of planned cases for an EDLP should be split across all months of the year. 

It's working correctly in Broker Promotional Management. It might be because it's still pulling from the TSS not Approved?  Same is happening in the Monthly Report. 

It’s causing issues for Bubbies who is looking to review their financial records and 338,502 Planned cases are only showing up in January just for 1 account.
