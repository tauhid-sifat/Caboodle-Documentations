## Description

**Issue 1: **The "Method of Entry" filter in both **Email Activity** and **Email History** sections currently includes a "Manual" option. This should be removed, since manually added entries do not actually trigger any email being sent to the user — making "Manual" an invalid/irrelevant filter option in the context of email activity/history.

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Go to Email Activity section > open "Method of Entry" filter.
2. Observe "Manual" listed as an option.
3. Go to Email History section > open "Method of Entry" filter.
4. Observe "Manual" listed as an option there as well.

**Expected Behavior:**
"Manual" should not appear as an option in the "Method of Entry" filter in either Email Activity or Email History, since manually added entries never result in an email being sent — making this option irrelevant/misleading in this context.

**Actual Behavior:**
"Manual" is currently present as a filter option in both sections.
