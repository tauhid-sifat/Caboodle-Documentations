## Description

The Stepper would be more efficient by utilizing the data from the Distributor reports.

Example, if a User wants to create a promotion for the Banner Bristol Farms, they must go to the UNFI or KeHE report, Filter by the Banner, export the data from the report, open it and manually enter that information in step 2 (Products)

Here is example of Bristol Farms from the UNFI Report

!image-20260410-133150.png|width=486,alt="image-20260410-133150.png"!

This data should be pre-populated in Step 2 Products

!image-20260410-133201.png|width=468,alt="image-20260410-133201.png"!

Expected Result:

·       Blueberry Lavender would automatically populate *Average Unit Velocity* as *10.2* & *Stores 5*

·       This would apply to every item from the distributor report

·       User should be allowed to enter additional items from the list that might not be pre-populated because they may have a different status than Active.
