## Description

**Steps to Reproduce:**

1. Go to **Add New Event**.
2. Select a banner (e.g., *Haggen*).
3. Proceed to Step 2 (Products).
4. Check the category list.

**Observed Behavior:**

* **Case 1:** Category 5 is displayed even though it has no products.
* **Case 2:** Category 6 is displayed even though its product only exists under the *Amazon* banner, not the selected *Haggen* banner.
