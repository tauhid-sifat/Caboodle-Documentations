## Description

## Summary

On the Simple Promotion entry grid, a line whose product has **no price** cannot be submitted. The grid shows a blocking error and the event is not created.

Per the story CS-1708, a Simple Promotion must **not** require a per-unit price. Creating an event for an unpriced product is expected to succeed. This is the core purpose of the feature.

## Environment

Staging, brand **Apple Jacks**, Promotional Planning → Simple Promotion entry

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Simple Promotion entry for brand Apple Jacks.
2. On the row for **ABSCO / ABSCO National / Sparkling Water / Natural Sparkling Mineral Water 750 ml Glass** (UPC 858072005290), which has no price list entry, enter Start Date **01/01** and End Date **01/16**.
3. Submit.

## Actual result

A banner reads "1 cell needs attention before this can be submitted", followed by:

> ABSCO National · Natural Sparkling Mineral Water 750 ml Glass — This product cannot carry a promotion. No price is set for this product. **Set price**

The row is greyed out and flagged. Submission is blocked and no event is created.

## Expected result

* Submission succeeds with 200, and the event is created.
* The promotion appears in Brand Approved with `entryType: 10`.
* On the details screen, the costed fields (`caseCost`, `caseCostMarkup`, `retailerNetCase`, `netUnitRetailerCost`, `retailerProfit`) are returned as **null**, and `eventsWithoutPricing` counts the row.

## Notes for dev

* Please check whether the block comes from FE-side grid validation or from `POST tradespends/simple/validate` / `POST tradespends/simple`, using the network response.
* The QA guide explicitly lists "Requires product to have a per unit price: **no**" for Simple Promotions.
* Related QA references: §4.10, §5.2.1–5.2.6.
