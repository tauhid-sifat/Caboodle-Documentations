## Description

When a Promotion is copied in Trade Spend it does not populate the Promotional Calendar.  

Additionally when new items are added to a copied promotion, it uses the old pricing if there was a change in the prior year.  Example if you copy an promotion from 2024 to 2025 and the Active date is September 2024, it takes the pricing from that date.  The pricing changed in October 2024 but this is for 2025.
