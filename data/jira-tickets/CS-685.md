## Description

Stepper > Step 6 > Apply to All > Ad/TPR Fee > Enter total Fee for the event. Automatically divide it across all skus selected for the event.  

Example: There are 5 skus included in the selected event.  User enters 500 in the Ad/TPR Fee field and $100 is applied to each sku of the 5 Skus in the Promo Details on the Promo Event Page
