## Description

As A Broker, one of the most important functions is ensure that my brands have the opportunity to present their products to the retailers. Retailers only review product categories once a year, so if a brand misses the deadline to present, they have to wait for another year. It can be difficult and tedious for a broker to keep track of this process for every brand. 

Currently in the Caboodle Brand Environment there is a Category Review Calendar. Brands have the option to add Retailer Submission details to that Calendar. It would be useful for the broker if the Calendars from the brand environment rolled up to a central calendar on the Broker Dashboard. This would be similar to how the Promotional Calendars for each brand rolls up to the Promotional Management Module on the Broker Dashboard. That way the Broker doesn’t have to go into each individual environment to check submissions.
