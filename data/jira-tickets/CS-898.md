## Description

Linked Product list in Category creation shows all products disabled without clear messaging.

* *Steps to Reproduce:*
*# Go to "Create Category" or edit an existing one.
*# Click on "Linked Products" dropdown.
* *Actual Result:*
** All products are shown but disabled from selection.
** No tooltip or message explains why they cannot be selected.
* *Expected Result:*
** Products already linked to other categories should be marked clearly (e.g., greyed out with tooltip: “Already linked to another category”).
** Or ideally, only show eligible products to prevent confusion.
