## Description

## Summary

On the Simple Promotion entry screen, the **Plan year** dropdown only offers a fixed 4-year window, **2025 to 2028**. Years outside that window, such as 2024 or 2029, cannot be selected.

The backend accepts any plan year from **2000 to 2100**. Per the QA guide §4.6, only years outside that range return 400: "Please choose a plan year between 2000 and 2100." The FE is therefore stricter than the API and blocks valid entries.

## Environment

Staging, brand **Apple Jacks**, Promotional Planning → Simple Promotion

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Promotional Planning → Simple Promotion → Add New Promotion.
2. Open the **Plan year** dropdown.

## Actual result

* Only 2025, 2026, 2027 and 2028 are listed.
* 2024 and earlier years are not available.
* 2029 and later years are not available.

## Expected result

* The Plan year options should align with the backend's validation range (2000 to 2100), or with an agreed product range.
* At minimum, the user should be able to pick past years (for historical or back-dated entries) and future years beyond +2.
* The rule should be consistent with the plan-year selection used elsewhere in Trade Spend.

## Notes

* The backend returns 400 for years outside 2000 to 2100. The FE should either match that range or show the same validation message.
* If a narrower range is intended, please confirm it with the PO and document it in CS-1708.
