## Description

In the Monthly Report section, when no filters are applied (default/unfiltered view), the *Total Plan Cases* and *Total Revenue* values shown in the graph do not match the corresponding totals in the table below/alongside it.

*Impact*

* Users cannot trust which number (graph vs. table) is accurate.
* Potential for incorrect business decisions if the wrong total is relied upon.
* Raises doubt about the reliability of other metrics/reports in the platform.

*Steps to Reproduce*

# Navigate to Monthly Report section.
# Do not apply any filters (default view).
# Compare *Total Plan Cases* value shown on the graph vs. the total in the table.
# Compare *Total Revenue* value shown on the graph vs. the total in the table.
# Observe the discrepancy between the two.

*Expected Behavior*

* Graph totals and table totals should always match when representing the same dataset and filter state (including the default/no-filter state).

*Actual Behavior*

* Total Plan Cases and Total Revenue differ between the graph and the table when no filters are applied.
