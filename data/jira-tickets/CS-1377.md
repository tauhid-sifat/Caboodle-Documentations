## Description

When an admin clicks on the *“Resend Invitation”* button for an invited user, no new invitation email is received in the user’s inbox.

*Steps to Reproduce:*

# Invite a user via email
# Go to the invited user list/admin section
# Click on the *“Resend Invitation”* button
# Check the invited user’s inbox

*Actual Behavior:*
No new invitation email is received after clicking the resend button.

*Expected Behavior:*
A new invitation email should be triggered and delivered successfully to the user’s inbox when the admin clicks on *“Resend Invitation.”*
