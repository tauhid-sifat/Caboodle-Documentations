## Description

Currently the system is set to turn the graphs in the Trade Spend Results Page Red when it reached 15.1% or above. Is there a way for Admin, Brand or otherwise a way control at what % rate it turns red? 

For example, a brand may have a budget of 20% and would prefer that the graph doesn’t turn red until the value is 20.1% or grater
